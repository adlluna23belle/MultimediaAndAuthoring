Rotoscoping

Rotoscoping is the technique of animating by tracing over live-action video footage frame by frame. Originally patented in 1917, it has evolved into a digital animation method used for realistic motion and stylized effects. This project documents the full creative process from raw footage to final animation output.

Final Output

Final Video of Rotoscope Project

The final output of rotoscoping animation is the result of frame-by-frame tracing, coloring, and editing using Adobe After Effects, Clip Studio, and CapCut.
